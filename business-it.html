A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

Do you provide ongoing support?

We can discuss ongoing care where it fits your needs. The scope, availability and price must be agreed; this page does not promise a 24-hour help desk or an emergency response time.

Do we need to move to Microsoft or Google?

Not necessarily. We look at what you already use, what works and what needs to change before recommending a platform.

Can you help with a specific problem?

Yes. Tell us about it directly. You do not need a check-up first. If the issue is outside the work we can take on, we will explain that.

What is getting in the way?

Tell us about your email, files, devices or team setup.
