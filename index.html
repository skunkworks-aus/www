Fair questions.

Is the check-up really free?

Yes. No cost and no obligation. It’s how we get to know your business.

Will you try to sell me something?

We’ll tell you what we’d fix and roughly what it would cost. If you’d rather do it yourself or with someone else, the report is still yours.

We’re a tiny business. Is it worth it?

Yes. Small teams often get the most out of a few simple fixes, because nobody has had time to look.

Do we need to be on Microsoft 365?

No. Plenty of local businesses are, and we know it well, but we’ll work with whatever you use now.

How long does it take?

Usually about an hour at your business. You can keep working while we look.

Where do you work?

Colac and the wider Colac Otway area. If you’re further out, ask anyway.
