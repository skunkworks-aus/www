A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

How long does it take?

We agree the visit length and a suitable time with you when arranging your check-up.

Will you change our settings during the check-up?

The review is about understanding your setup. Any changes, access or agreed quick-win work are discussed with you first. Never send us passwords in an enquiry.

Does requesting a time confirm a booking?

No. We will contact you to agree the arrangements. You do not have a confirmed appointment until we agree it with you.
