A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you enquire.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. We first look at how it is built and what you want to change, then explain what can be improved or whether a rebuild makes more sense.

What will it cost?

The quote depends on the pages, content and functionality. We agree the scope and explain one-off and ongoing costs before you commit.

Do I have to start with a check-up?

No. If you know you need a website, enquire directly. The free check-up is there if you want help deciding where to start.

Who supplies the words and photos?

We agree that in the proposal. Bring any existing content, photos and brand material; we will identify what else is needed.
