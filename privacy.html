How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Contact Luca ↗Call 0493 240 070Email Luca ↗

Website enquiries

When you submit an enquiry, your name, email address or phone number, message and any optional details you provide are sent to FormSubmit. FormSubmit processes the submission and emails it to Skunkworks Australia at info@skunkworksau.com so we can respond.

Please include only what is needed to discuss your enquiry. Do not send passwords, customer records or other sensitive information.

Form delivery and storage

The website does not have its own enquiry database. FormSubmit handles delivery and states that its submission archive is retained for 30 days. Email copies are handled by our email provider and may remain in our business mailbox.

See FormSubmit’s privacy information and submission and archive documentation. You can call or email us directly if you prefer not to use the form.

Your display preference

If you change the light or dark theme, we remember that choice in this browser using local storage. Site search filters our page links on your device; search terms are not sent to a search provider.

Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information to deliver the site. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Fonts and design assets are served with this website. We do not include advertising trackers or website analytics.

Email, phone and external links

Direct email and phone links open the relevant app on your device. Email delivery is handled by the providers used by you and Skunkworks Australia. External services have their own information-handling practices.

Questions

Contact info@skunkworksau.com about the details you have shared or to ask about correction or deletion.
