FAIR QUESTIONS

Before you get in touch.

Is the check-up really free?

Yes. There is no cost and no obligation to buy further work. It helps us understand your business and recommend useful next steps.

Will you try to sell me something?

We explain the findings and can quote for work you want help with. If you prefer to do it yourself or use someone else, the report is still yours.

We’re a tiny business. Is it worth it?

Yes. We work with small businesses and can start with the practical problems that matter to your team.

Do we need to be on Microsoft 365?

No. We look at the tools you already use before recommending changes.

How long does a check-up take?

We agree the visit length and a suitable time with you when arranging your check-up.

Where do you work?

Colac and the wider Colac Otway area. If you are further out, ask about arrangements for your location.
