What we do.

One local team for the tech your business runs on. Start anywhere: most people begin with a free check-up and go from there.

  • Microsoft 365 & everyday IT

    Email, Teams, OneDrive, licences, new laptops and the “why won’t it print” days. Set up properly, then looked after.

    • Email on your own business domain, moved across from old accounts
    • Teams, OneDrive and SharePoint set up so files are shared properly
    • Licences checked so you only pay for what you use
    • New computers set up, old ones wiped safely
    • Help when something stops working

  • Online presence

    Websites, Google Business Profile, bookings and online shops. Easy for locals to find, easy for you to update.

    • Simple, fast websites you can update yourself
    • Google Business Profile set up and kept current
    • Online bookings and enquiry forms
    • Online shops, including Shopify

  • Automation & AI

    Hand the repetitive jobs to software, and use AI tools in a way that’s safe for your business.

    • Automatic reminders, follow-ups and confirmations
    • Apps connected so nothing gets typed in twice
    • Templates for quotes, invoices and common emails
    • AI assistants set up with sensible rules for your staff

  • Security

    Two-step sign-in, password managers, backups you’ve tested, and staff who can spot a scam email before it costs you.

    • Two-step sign-in on email, banking and key accounts
    • A password manager for the whole team
    • Backups set up and actually tested
    • Scam and phishing awareness for staff
    • A simple plan for what to do if something goes wrong

  • Skunkworks Academy

    Practical IT training for your team, and a pathway for anyone starting out in tech.

    • Hands-on training in Microsoft 365 and staying safe online
    • Courses, labs and certification pathways
    • Run through skunkworksacademy.com
